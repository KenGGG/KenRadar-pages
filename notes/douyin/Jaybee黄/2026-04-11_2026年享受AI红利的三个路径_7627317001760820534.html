一、内容简述

（一）一句话结论

一句话结论 · 内容来源的摘要 视频提出在AI时代保持领先需做到三点：第一，找到自己的unfair advantage（独特优势）并加倍投入；第二，以目标为导向使用AI作为杠杆而非被动等待；第三，每天深度使用各类AI工具，培养对其能力边界的直觉，从而形成独特视角。

（二）核心内容

  1. 核心观点：AI时代的制胜关键 自2025年12月起AI能力持续提升，很多人担心落后。视频认为决定上限的不是对AI工具的熟练程度，而是人对自己的觉知。

  2. 第一路径：找到并放大你的unfair advantage Unfair advantage指你超越他人的独特优势，能让你进入前1%。AI能处理大量平庸工作，如果你只能提供平庸内容就会被淘汰。找到优势后需double down并spike于其上。

  3. 第二路径：以目标为导向掌控AI 明确你想要达成的目标，用各种技能和工具去实现，让AI成为实现目标的杠杆。做那个有目的地控制AI的人，而不是局限于狭小范围被动等待AI进步。

  4. 第三路径：每日深度使用AI工具 每天高频使用Codex、Claude Code、Kimi Code等工具进行不同类型工作。目的是培养对工具局限性的深层直觉——知道它们在哪里会犯错。这种mental backlog会形成独特视角，与Code泄漏的经验类似，放大模型能力的关键在于对模型能力的深度理解。

  5. 总结 当AI承担所有执行工作后，剩下的思考就是人的思考，体现的是个人品味和方法。坚持三点：找到自己的优势、有目标地用AI、尽可能多使用AI工具，就能超越大多数人。

1. 引言：AI进步与不被落伍的三件事

So, starting from December 2025 last year, AI is getting better every single day, and a lot of people have been constantly asking me this question, how do I not get left behind? I've been thinking about it recently, and I think it all comes down to three things. If you can do these three things right, of course they are all difficult, you might be able to stay on top, and then you won't be just surviving, you're gonna thrive. 第一件事，我认为最重要的事，你必须要准备到一点点——准备到你的unfair advantage。

2. 第一件事：找到你的unfair advantage

所以，什么是unfair advantage？订阅所有的技术，在每个游戏中，你会有各种不同的资源。所以，你的unfair advantage是，你能处理到每个游戏，you can outperform them by a large scale, by a large margin, and you can easily get to the 99 percentile. So that is your unfair advantage, and once you find it, you're going to double down on it, so try to spike on that unfair advantage. And the reason why is AI is able to take care of a lot of mediocre works, and if the only thing that you bring to the table is mediocrity, you know, mediocre writing——当然，AI可转换你。但是，当你遇到你的unfair advantage，这些东西是人们可以直接接触到。你能够用AI来积极，你能够伴你的unfair advantage。

3. 第二件事：用工具达成目标

第二件事是，你想要弄弄弄弄……在那些地方有工具，工具，工具。You're going to use whatever skill or tools to achieve your goals, and AI will become that leverage to goal. So you are essentially becoming the person who controls AI with a purpose, instead of, you know, just staying in a very narrow scope and waiting for AI to catch up to you.

4. 第三件事：日常深度使用AI工具

And the third thing which I think is the most practical is to stay on top of the tools, which means you want to use the AI tools daily, deeply, regularly. And you can use whatever AI tools you know——Codex, Claude Code, Kimi Code, Claude Code Work, whatever——just try to use them every day intensively in different kinds of work because they're not only capable of coding now. And the reason why you need to do that is you want to develop a deep intuition of the limitations of these tools——where do they stumble? And once you develop this mental backloop, the benefit is you're going to have a unique perspective of what AI can do and can't do, and your perspective can be very deep. And you know, because like eventually AI is going to be able to execute a lot of the tasks, and what differentiates you from the other people is you have a unique perspective using AI. One example is recently Claude had their code leaked, and if you check out the repository, most of the experience of Claude Code actually comes from harnessing——like the model's capability is already pretty good, but the way to amplify model capability is this human-level harnessing, and harness actually comes from a deep understanding of the capability of the model.

5. 总结：培养直觉与品味

So use them daily and try to develop these deep intuitions. Because basically, when AI担当了所有的努力，所剩下的工作——所剩下的思考是人的思考，而且基本上是你的口味。So your taste matters. So the method is——is to use these tools. So three things: First, try your unfair advantage. Second, try to use tools. Third, try to use AI tools as much as possible. If you can do these things, you will stand out from everyone else. Stay on top.
